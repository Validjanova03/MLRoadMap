Bias
* Difference between Predicted and Actual vales. (High difference = oversimplified model)
* Oversimplified data cause NOT recofnizing data patterns or complexities
* Underfitting = High Bias, Low Variance 

Variance
* Variability of Prediction
* Memorizing training data insted of memorizing patterns or complecities
* Overfitting = High Variance, Low Bias

We want Low Bias and Low Variance = We want a model that is able to recognize complexities and patterns in the training data but also on data that has NOT seen!
